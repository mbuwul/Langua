# A1.12 Review & A1 Exam Project (Herhaling en examenproject A1)

## Unit at a glance

This is the last unit of Level A1 (about 12 hours). First you review everything from A1.1 to A1.11 in one connected story, with a grammar overview and a full pronunciation check. Then you sit the **A1 exam project**: a mock exam built like the **Basisexamen Inburgering Buitenland**, the exam people take before they move to the Netherlands as a partner or family member. It has a speaking test, a reading test and a test on Dutch society (KNM). Two extra parts, writing and listening, check that you're ready for A2.

**You will be able to:**

- Use all A1 grammar together: present tense, verb second, questions, modals, separable verbs, adjectives, imperatives
- Pass a full pronunciation check of all Dutch sounds at word and sentence level
- Answer questions, repeat sentences, give opposites and retell a short story in Dutch
- Read short practical texts (signs, letters, ads) and answer questions about them
- Answer 30 basic questions about life in the Netherlands
- Know whether you are ready to book the real exam, and what to practise if not

**Builds on:** everything from A0.1 to A1.11.

## Part 1: Situation

### One week in Utrecht

Grace tells her taalmaatje about her week. Every sentence uses something from A1. Listen, then read along.

> **Maandag** sta ik om half zeven op. Ik breng Leo naar school en daarna fiets ik naar het ziekenhuis. Ik werk van negen tot vier.
>
> **Dinsdag** is Leo ziek. Hij heeft koorts en hij hoest. Ik bel de huisarts. De assistente zegt: "Geef hem paracetamol en laat hem veel drinken." Ik meld Leo ziek bij juf Anouk.
>
> **Woensdag** gaat het beter met Leo. 's Middags ga ik naar de gemeente, want ik moet een nieuwe ID-kaart aanvragen. Ik moet een nummertje trekken en een half uur wachten.
>
> **Donderdag** heb ik om half drie een afspraak bij de kapper. Daarna koop ik een warme winterjas. Hij is in de aanbieding!
>
> **Vrijdag** gaan mijn collega's naar de borrel. Ik drink een kopje thee en ik blijf een uurtje. Het is heel gezellig.
>
> **Zaterdag** komen mijn schoonouders. Mijn schoonmoeder neemt een taart mee. Er zijn geen stoelen genoeg, dus Tom haalt twee stoelen bij de buren.
>
> **Zondag** doen we niets. We slapen uit, we wandelen in het park en 's avonds kijken we een film. Ik vind zondag de leukste dag van de week!

**Check questions:**

1. Why does Grace phone the doctor on Tuesday? (a) she's ill (b) Leo is ill (c) she needs an appointment for Tom. **Answer: b**
2. What does Grace do at the town hall? (a) register a move (b) apply for an ID card (c) pick up a passport. **Answer: b**
3. What does Grace buy on Thursday? (a) a winter coat (b) a cake (c) chairs. **Answer: a**
4. What does Grace drink at the borrel? (a) beer (b) wine (c) tea. **Answer: c**
5. Why does Tom go to the neighbours? (a) to borrow chairs (b) to bring cake (c) to invite them. **Answer: a**
6. Which day does Grace like best? (a) Friday (b) Saturday (c) Sunday. **Answer: c**

**Grammar hunt:** find in the story one example of each: a separable verb, a modal verb with an infinitive, an imperative, er zijn, a diminutive, an adjective with -e, verb second after a time word. (Separable: sta op, meld ziek, neemt mee, slapen uit. Modal: moet aanvragen, moet trekken. Imperative: Geef, laat. Er zijn: Er zijn geen stoelen. Diminutive: kopje, uurtje, nummertje. Adjective -e: warme winterjas, nieuwe ID-kaart. Verb second: 's Middags ga ik, Daarna koop ik, Daarna fiets ik.)

## Part 3: Grammar

### 3A. The A1 grammar overview

| Topic | The rule in one line | Example | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pronouns | ik, jij/je, u, hij, zij/ze, het, wij/we, jullie, zij/ze | Wij wonen in Gent. | A1.1 |
| zijn and hebben | irregular: ben/bent/is/zijn, heb/hebt/heeft/hebben | Hij heeft twee kinderen. | A1.1 |
| de and het | learn the article; plurals and -je words follow rules | het kind, de kinderen | A1.2 |
| Plurals | -en, -s, 's, with spelling rules | zussen, vaders, opa's | A1.2 |
| Possessives | mijn, je/jouw, uw, zijn, haar, ons/onze, jullie, hun | onze zoon, ons kind | A1.2 |
| Present tense | stem, stem + t, infinitive | ik werk, hij werkt, wij werken | A1.3 |
| Verb second | the verb is always element 2 | Om acht uur begin ik. | A1.3 |
| Questions | verb first; jij loses -t | Werk je vandaag? | A1.4 |
| niet and geen | geen before een-words and plurals | Ik heb geen auto. | A1.4 |
| er is / er zijn | there is / there are | Er zijn twee slaapkamers. | A1.5 |
| Prepositions | in, op, aan, onder, naast, tussen... | Het schilderij hangt aan de muur. | A1.5 |
| Modal verbs | modal second, infinitive at the end | Ik wil een kaartje kopen. | A1.6 |
| Separable verbs | verb second, prefix at the end | Ik sta om zeven uur op. | A1.7 |
| Dates | op + day, in + month, om + time | op maandag 3 mei om tien uur | A1.7 |
| Adjectives | -e, except een + het-word | een groot huis, de grote jas | A1.8 |
| hij/het for things | de-word = hij, het-word = het | De broek? Hij is te klein. | A1.8 |
| Imperative | stem, verb first | Neem twee tabletten. | A1.9 |
| van + person | whose | Die is van mij. | A1.9 |
| graag/liever/liefst | after the verb | Ik zwem graag. | A1.10 |
| Diminutives | -je, -tje, -pje, -etje, -kje; always het | een kopje koffie | A1.10 |
| u forms | u keeps the -t | Woont u hier? | A1.11 |

## Part 8: Culture & KNM review

### What you should know about the Netherlands (A1 level)

The exam's KNM part (Kennis van de Nederlandse Samenleving, knowledge of Dutch society) asks simple questions about daily life, rules and values. Here is the core.

| Topic | Key facts |
| --- | --- |
| Geography | About 18 million people live in the Netherlands. There are 12 provinces. About a quarter of the country is below sea level, protected by dikes and dunes. |
| Capital and government | Amsterdam is the capital. The government and parliament are in The Hague (Den Haag). |
| Royal family | The Netherlands is a constitutional monarchy. King Willem-Alexander has been king since 2013. Queen Máxima was born in Argentina. |
| Democracy | Parliament has two chambers: the Tweede Kamer (150 members) and the Eerste Kamer (75). You can vote from 18. Non-EU residents may vote in municipal elections after five years of legal residence. |
| Constitution | Article 1: everyone in the Netherlands must be treated equally in equal cases. Discrimination is forbidden. |
| Freedoms | Freedom of religion, freedom of speech, and separation of church and state. Men and women have equal rights. Same-sex marriage has been legal since 2001, the first country in the world. |
| Education | School is compulsory from age 5 to 16, and young people must then study until they have a basic qualification or turn 18. Primary school runs from age 4 to 12. |
| Health | Everyone must have health insurance. You go to the GP (huisarts) first. Emergency: 112. |
| Work and money | You need a BSN to work. There is a legal minimum wage. Most people pay tax through their salary. |
| Living | Register at the gemeente within five days of moving. Recycle waste: paper, glass and plastic. Respect quiet hours with neighbours. |
| Holidays | King's Day (27 April), Remembrance Day (4 May, two minutes' silence at 8 pm), Liberation Day (5 May), Christmas, Easter. |
| Manners | Be on time, make appointments, shake hands, and say what you think, politely but directly. |

## Part 9: A1 exam project

### About the real exam

The **Basisexamen Inburgering Buitenland** is taken at a Dutch embassy or consulate, on a computer, before you can get a residence permit as a partner or family member of someone living in the Netherlands. It tests Dutch at level **A1** and has three parts: **speaking**, **reading** (including basic literacy) and **knowledge of Dutch society (KNM)**. There is no writing part.

**Before you book:** exam formats, times and prices change. Check the official site **naarnederland.nl** and the **inburgeren.nl** pages on civic integration abroad for the current format, the free practice exams and the official study pack "Naar Nederland". This mock follows the structure of those official materials as far as we can tell; when a detail differs, follow the official site.

**How to use this project:** do the parts in one session, without notes and without pausing, exactly as on the day. Then check your answers with the keys and the marking guides. **Pass every part at 70% or more** before booking the real exam.
